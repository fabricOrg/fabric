An accepted API response means Fabric owns the work — not that the handset received the message.
Delivery is asynchronous and reported after the request returns, through
[webhooks](/docs/webhooks/) and the retrieve endpoints.

## Direct SMS lifecycle

A direct SMS moves through the message pipeline:

`accepted` → `queued` → `sending` → `sent` → `delivered`

It can instead reach a terminal `undelivered`, `failed`, or `expired`. `sent` means the carrier
accepted it; `delivered` means the network confirmed handset receipt.

## Managed delivery lifecycle

A managed delivery has no pre-dispatch queue states — its lifecycle is:

`accepted` → `processing` → `sent` → `delivered`

with terminal `undelivered`, `failed`, or `expired`. Each provider attempt is recorded in the
delivery's `attempts[]` timeline with its own `status`, `cost`, and `errorCode`.

## What a delivery report can and cannot tell you

Delivery receipts (DLRs) come from mobile networks, and their honesty varies by operator and route.

- **`sent` is not `delivered`.** Treat carrier acceptance as in-flight, not done.
- **Some routes never confirm.** On a few networks a message that truly arrived may sit at `sent`
  because the operator returns no positive DLR. Do not treat the absence of `delivered` as failure.
- **Reports can arrive late or out of order.** Design consumers so a later event never regresses a
  terminal state, and so a duplicate is a no-op.
- **`failed` carries an `errorCode`.** Branch on the stable code, not the human message.

:::tip[Sandbox is deterministic]
Sandbox uses the virtual delivery path, so states resolve immediately and predictably — ideal for
wiring up your webhook consumer before you touch a live route.
:::

## Follow the terminal state

```ts
const delivery = await fabric.messages.retrieveDelivery(id);
if (delivery.data.status === "delivered") {
  // safe to mark your workflow complete
}
```

Retrieve is a snapshot; webhooks are the push. In production, advance your workflow from the signed
terminal [webhook event](/docs/webhooks/events/) and use retrieve for reconciliation and support.
