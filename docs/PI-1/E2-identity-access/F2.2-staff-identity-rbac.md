# F2.2 — Staff identity + admin RBAC

**Epic:** E2 Identity · **Priority:** MUST · **Iteration:** 1–2
**Depends on:** F2.1 · **Blocks:** all E9 control-plane features

## Why
Operators are a different population from customers and wield god-mode powers; they must never live
in a customer account, and dangerous actions need extra friction. (`IDENTITY-SSO.md §13`.)

## Stories
- **US-2.2.1** — As an **operator**, I sign in through a separate staff realm with mandatory MFA, so internal access is isolated from customer auth.
- **US-2.2.2** — As a **platform owner**, staff have least-privilege roles, so support can't move money and finance can't change providers.
- **US-2.2.3** — As an **operator**, dangerous actions require step-up auth, so a hijacked session can't silently do harm.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Separate **staff org/realm** (not mixed with customer tenants); **MFA mandatory for all staff**.
- [ ] Roles: `super_admin · platform_ops · finance · support · compliance · read_only`, enforced per admin action.
- [ ] **Step-up auth** required for: wallet adjustments, refunds, impersonation, provider kill-switch.
- [ ] Admin console runs on an **isolated deployment** (`admin.*`), separate session/cookie, not publicly exposed.
- [ ] Test: a `support` staff cannot perform a `finance` action; step-up is enforced on a guarded action.

## Design notes
- Same OIDC/BFF pattern as customers but a distinct realm + ingress.
- Maker-checker (F9.4) layers on top for money operations.

## Out of scope (this PI)
- Fine-grained custom role builder; SCIM provisioning of staff.

## Definition of Done
- RBAC matrix test; step-up enforced on guarded actions; staff cannot authenticate into customer apps.
