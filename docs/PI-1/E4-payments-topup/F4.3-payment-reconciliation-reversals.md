# F4.3 — Payment reconciliation & reversals

**Epic:** E4 Payments · **Priority:** MUST · **Iteration:** 4
**Depends on:** F4.2, F3.1, F1.4 · **Blocks:** money correctness

## Why
Top-up money isn't final at "succeeded." Two real failure modes were unhandled: a webhook that
**never arrives** (top-up stuck `pending`), and a payment later **refunded/disputed/reversed** by the
provider after we already credited the wallet. Both corrupt the ledger if ignored.

## Stories
- **US-4.3.1** — As the **platform**, a top-up left `pending` is actively reconciled and resolved, so abandoned/lost-webhook payments don't hang forever.
- **US-4.3.2** — As the **platform**, when a credited payment is later refunded/disputed/reversed, the wallet is debited (clawed back), so we're never credited-but-unpaid.
- **US-4.3.3** — As a **finance operator**, I can see and resolve unreconciled/negative-balance cases.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] **Pending sweeper:** a scheduled job actively **verifies** top-ups still `pending` after a TTL (poll the provider); resolves to `succeeded` (credit once, idempotent) or `failed/expired` (no credit); audited.
- [ ] **Reversal handling:** a `payment.refunded | disputed | reversed` provider event posts a **compensating debit** (category `reversal`) to the ledger (F3.1), idempotent on the provider reference.
- [ ] **Negative-balance policy:** a reversal may drive balance negative; the wallet **may go negative** (clawback debt), new sends are blocked until cleared, and the case is flagged to operators.
- [ ] Reversal/clawback never edits prior entries — only compensating entries (immutability, F3.1).
- [ ] Ledger invariant (F1.5) holds across pending-resolve and reversal.
- [ ] Test: lost-webhook → sweeper credits once; reversal after spend → negative balance + sends blocked; duplicate reversal applies once.

## Design notes
- Webhook remains source of truth; the sweeper is the safety net for the never-arriving webhook (mirrors F3.3 for sends).
- Clawback/negative-balance recovery (collections) policy detail = fast-follow; PI-1 blocks sends + flags.

## Out of scope (this PI)
- Automated dunning/collections for negative balances; partial refunds proration UI.

## Definition of Done
- Pending-sweep, reversal-clawback, negative-balance-block, and idempotency tests green; invariant holds.
