# F6.3 — SMS-pumping / fraud protection

**Epic:** E6 OTP/Verify · **Priority:** MUST (basic) · **Iteration:** 4
**Depends on:** F6.1 · **Blocks:** —

## Why
SMS pumping (AIT) drains Verify margin and customer wallets via fraudulent OTP traffic to controlled
number ranges. Twilio ships Fraud Guard **on by default, free** — parity matters.

## Stories
- **US-6.3.1** — As a **tenant**, suspicious OTP destinations are blocked automatically, so I'm not billed for pumping fraud.
- **US-6.3.2** — As the **platform**, anomalous destination patterns are throttled/blocked by default, protecting margin.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Per-tenant + per-destination-prefix **rate/anomaly limits** on Verify sends, on by default.
- [ ] Destinations showing pumping patterns (volume spikes to specific prefixes/ranges) are blocked; block reason recorded.
- [ ] Blocked attempts are **not billed** (platform-fault exemption, F3.5) and are audited.
- [ ] Operators can view/adjust thresholds (F9.5).
- [ ] Test: a simulated pumping pattern (burst to one prefix) is throttled/blocked and not charged.

## Design notes
- Basic heuristic (prefix anomaly + velocity) in PI-1; risk-score/Lookup integration is PI-2.

## Out of scope (this PI)
- Lookup line-type / SIM-swap risk scoring; ML-based scoring.

## Definition of Done
- Pumping-pattern block test; blocked attempts excluded from billing.
